TOIL 01
測驗 離開
advocate 提倡者；擁護者；提倡、主張
She is a strong advocate of independent travel.
convinced 確信的；被說服的
I'm convinced that this is the best hotel in town.
pre-arranged 預先安排好的
Our airport pickup was pre-arranged by the travel agency.
for starters 首先；第一點（口語）
For starters, we need to decide where to go.
hassle 麻煩；困擾
Changing trains three times was a real hassle.
transportation 交通；運輸
The tour price includes all transportation.
accommodations 住宿
We booked our accommodations two months in advance.
guide 導遊；指南；引導
The guide showed us around the old town.
reservation 預約；預訂
Do you have a reservation for tonight?
leave ~ behind 把……留下；拋下；忘了帶走
Don't leave your umbrella behind on the bus.
frustration 挫折；沮喪
He shouted in frustration when his flight was canceled.
relaxing 令人放鬆的
We had a relaxing weekend at a hot spring.
efficient 有效率的
The new train system is clean and efficient.
independent 獨立的；自主的
My sister became independent after she started working.
around-the-clock 全天候的；24 小時的
The hospital provides around-the-clock care.
facilitate 促進；使便利
Good maps facilitate travel in a new city.
negotiation 談判；協商
After some negotiation, we got a better price.
red tape 繁文縟節；官僚程序
There is too much red tape when you apply for a work visa.
carefree 無憂無慮的
Children often live carefree lives.
organized 有組織的；有條理的
She is very organized and never forgets anything.
benefit 好處；利益；受益
One benefit of living here is the clean air.
independent travel 自助旅行
Independent travel requires a lot of planning.
pamper 寵愛；悉心照顧；縱容
She pampered herself with a day at the spa.
in addition 此外；另外
The room is big. In addition, it has a great view.
insurance 保險
Does your insurance cover lost luggage?
pickpocket 扒手；扒竊
Watch out for pickpockets in crowded places.
poisoning 中毒
Food poisoning can ruin a vacation.
privileged 有特權的；榮幸的；優越的
I feel privileged to work with such talented people.
translate 翻譯
Could you translate this sign for me?
rip off 敲竹槓；坑錢（名詞 rip-off）
That souvenir shop really ripped us off.
security 安全；保全；安檢
Hotel security checked our room key.
along 沿著；一起
Why don't you come along with us?
weirdo 怪人；怪胎
Everyone thought he was a weirdo, but he was actually very kind.
protect 保護
Locks help protect your bags from thieves.
for sure 確定地；肯定
I'll be there for sure.
comradery 同伴情誼；友情（= camaraderie）
There was great comradery among the team members.
festive 節慶的；歡樂的
The city looks very festive at Christmas.
at ease 自在；放鬆
She always feels at ease with her old friends.
keep ... out of 使……遠離；不讓……進入
Keep your phone out of the rain.
friendship 友誼
Their friendship has lasted for twenty years.
atmosphere 氣氛；大氣
The restaurant has a romantic atmosphere.
meal 一餐；餐點
We had a delicious meal by the sea.
lively 熱鬧的；活潑的
The bar was lively and full of music.
negative 負面的；消極的
Try not to be so negative about everything.
hogwash 胡說八道；廢話
He said the story was complete hogwash.
restrictive 限制性的；拘束的
The rules at the hostel were very restrictive.
in conclusion 總之；最後（總結用語）
In conclusion, traveling is the best way to learn.
the best of both worlds 兩全其美
Working from home gives me the best of both worlds.
go with the flow 順其自然；隨遇而安
I don't make plans on vacation. I just go with the flow.
obvious 明顯的
It was obvious that she was tired.
stereotype 刻板印象
It's a stereotype that all Americans eat fast food.
thrilling 令人興奮的；刺激的
The final minutes of the game were thrilling.
flexible 有彈性的；靈活的
My working hours are very flexible.
slip 滑倒；溜走；小失誤
I slipped on the ice and hurt my knee.
get the most out of 充分利用；從……獲得最大好處
Take notes to get the most out of the class.
