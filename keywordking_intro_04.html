單字王 04
測驗 離開
sententious 說教的；愛講大道理的；（言辭）簡潔精闢的
His speeches were long and sententious, full of moral advice.
entitle 給予……權利（資格）；為（書、文章）命名
This ticket entitles you to one free drink.
heap 一堆；大量；堆積
There was a heap of dirty clothes on the floor.
courtesy 禮貌；禮節；好意（courtesy of 承蒙……提供）
It's common courtesy to say thank you.
vast 巨大的；廣闊的；大量的
The desert is vast and empty.
satellite 衛星；人造衛星
The TV signal comes from a satellite.
souvenir 紀念品
I bought a small souvenir for my mom in Kyoto.
peak 山頂；頂峰；高峰期
We reached the peak of the mountain at sunrise.
handful 一把；少數
Only a handful of people came to the meeting.
decade 十年
She has lived in Taipei for over a decade.
leather 皮革；皮製的
He wore a black leather jacket.
clothe 給……穿衣；供給衣服
They work hard to feed and clothe their children.
recognition 認出；承認；表揚、肯定
She received recognition for her years of hard work.
hose 水管；軟管；用水管沖洗
He washed the car with a garden hose.
literature 文學；文獻
She studied English literature in college.
pace 步調；速度；踱步
Life in the countryside moves at a slower pace.
missive 信件；公文（正式或帶幽默的說法）
She sent a long missive to her old friend.
sharpen 削尖；磨利；使敏銳
I need to sharpen my pencil before the test.
violation 違反；侵犯
Parking here is a violation of the rules.
