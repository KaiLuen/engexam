單字王 02
測驗 離開
fetch 去拿來；取來
Could you fetch me a glass of water?
catalog 目錄；型錄
I ordered a new jacket from the online catalog.
bend 彎曲；彎腰
She bent down to pick up the coin.
perfume 香水
She bought a bottle of perfume for her mother.
rumour 謠言；傳聞
There is a rumour that the company will close next year.
qualification 資格；學歷證書
He has the right qualifications for the job.
operation 手術；運作；操作
My grandfather had an operation on his knee last month.
praise 稱讚；讚美
The teacher praised her for her excellent work.
absent 缺席的
Tom was absent from school yesterday because he was sick.
dodger 逃避者；躲避者
The police caught a fare dodger on the train.
manner 方式；態度；（複數）禮貌
It is bad manners to talk with your mouth full.
storm 暴風雨
The storm knocked down many trees last night.
thick 厚的；濃的
This book is too thick to finish in one day.
scene 場景；現場
The police arrived at the scene of the accident quickly.
humble 謙虛的；卑微的
Despite his success, he remains humble.
burst 爆裂；突然發生
The balloon burst when it touched the candle.
careless 粗心的
It was careless of me to leave the door unlocked.
bread 麵包
I had bread and milk for breakfast.
frankly 坦白地；老實說
Frankly, I don't think this plan will work.
